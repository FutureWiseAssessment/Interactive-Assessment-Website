FutureWise updated assessment package

Changes:
- Part 1 course recommendations now use academic scores only.
- A selected academic subject must reach 70% to generate Part 1 course recommendations.
- Lower-scoring subjects are ignored for Part 1 recommendations.
- Example: Math 3/10 and Language 8/10 => only Language-related Part 1 courses are recommended.
- TVL/TechPro recommendations were removed from Part 1.
- Part 2 now presents more college courses, related careers, scholarship opportunity categories, and TVL/TechPro pathways.
- Part 2 order: College Courses -> Careers -> Scholarships -> TVL/TechPro.
- The existing CAM HIGH image/theme and assessment questions are preserved.
